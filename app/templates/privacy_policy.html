About Daboclip

{{ app_name }} (Daboclip) is a personal / local video processing tool. The application is used primarily by its developer for personal use — it is not a marketplace, not a public SaaS, has no advertising system, and does not sell user data.

Data Processed

Video, audio, and transcripts you provide are processed primarily locally on the computer running the application. Analysis results, candidate clips, and short videos are also processed and stored locally.

Certain features (such as Large Language Model analysis) may send portions of data — such as transcript text — to third-party AI providers when enabled, according to your configured preferences.

What We Do Not Do With Your Data

  • Daboclip does not sell user data to third parties.
  • Daboclip does not use data for advertising or ad targeting.
  • Daboclip does not collect data for commercial purposes.
  • Without your explicit instruction, no video/audio data is uploaded to any public service by the app.

Third-Party Integrations

Third-party integrations — such as TikTok or Instagram APIs to assist publishing — are only used for features explicitly triggered by the user. The application does not activate these integrations without your action.

  • Platform data is used only for integrations you authorize (such as publishing videos on your request).
  • Tokens or authorization credentials are handled securely — stored locally, never shared, and never used outside the authorized integration features.
  • Third-party platform data practices (TikTok, Meta, etc.) are governed by their respective privacy policies, not this policy.
